# Components

## Thumbnail templates are editable defaults

Thumbnail presets provide defaults, never locked content. Keep logo choice, headline font, copy, and uploaded image layers independently editable; every preset must support a background-only state. Preview presenter/product imagery may demonstrate a composition, but do not bake it into editable content unless it is approved background artwork.
