## Preserve Page 4 thumbnail recipes exactly

Named Page 4 YouTube templates use their source-frame background, logo geometry, typography, alignment, spacing, effects, and image slots. Instrument Sans/zero tracking remains the generic editor default only; template-specific Instrument Serif, SemiCondensed, Rajdhani, and source tracking overrides are intentional.

## Do not bake content images into templates

Template previews may show source artwork, but live editable canvases may pre-populate only logos. People, screenshots, product art, and other content imagery must remain empty image slots added by users.
