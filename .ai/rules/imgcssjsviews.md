## Use supplied Laravel Open Graph system

Laravel Open Graph cards use public/img/laravel-og-background.png (1800x945 source for 1200x630 output) with public/img/laravel-logo.png. Keep the white left field, blue grid panel, gray guide, and supplied red-orange side tab intact. Laravel thumbnails retain the red thumbnail treatment and icon mark.
